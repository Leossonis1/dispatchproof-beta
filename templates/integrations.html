The DispatchProof Role

Your back office tracks the job. DispatchProof runs it.

DispatchProof is built for the operational handoff between the office and the jobsite: deployment, crews, subcontractors, readiness, documents, field updates, conflicts, and completion.

DispatchProof Works Standalone

Quickbase—or any other ERP/back-office platform—is optional.

If your company does not use Quickbase, nothing changes. DispatchProof remains a complete field-execution workspace for the operational side of installation work.

The integration framework is intentionally separate from everyday job execution so future connectors do not add clutter for companies that do not need them.

Future Connectors

Built to stay vendor-neutral.

The same handoff model can support other back-office systems later when real customer demand justifies a connector.

Product boundary

DispatchProof will not try to become your accounting package, estimating system, or ERP. Integrations should move operational job information in and completion information out—not recreate the mothership inside the field tool.

Quickbase is a third-party product. DispatchProof is not affiliated with or endorsed by Quickbase.
