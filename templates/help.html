New to DispatchProof?

The basic flow is simple: create the job, confirm readiness, record installer arrival, then complete and report the install.

1. Create Job → 2. Readiness → 3. Arrival → 4. Complete → 5. Report
Where DispatchProof Fits

Your back office tracks the job. DispatchProof runs it.

DispatchProof is the field-execution layer for deployment, crews, subcontractors, readiness, documents, conflicts, and job completion. It is designed to work on its own or beside the accounting, estimating, ERP, or business system your company already uses.

Back Office→DispatchProof→Back Office

Keep estimating, purchase orders, invoicing, accounting, and financial closeout in the system where they already belong. DispatchProof owns the operational work in the middle.

Basics

Getting Started

The minimum you need to run an installation through DispatchProof.

01

Use Needs Attention

  1. Open Dashboard.
  2. Review the Needs Attention panel before the full job list.
  3. DispatchProof prioritizes overdue installs, today's installs, Blocked / Review jobs, and No Response jobs inside the next 7 days.
  4. For a No Response item, click Generate Reminder directly from the priority queue when follow-up is needed.
  5. If a reminder already exists, DispatchProof shows the reminder count / latest time and changes the action to Generate Another Reminder.
  6. Confirm the repeat-reminder warning before generating another follow-up.
  7. Use Open Job → for the full installation record and other actions.
Reminder history is based on the Email Outbox / delivery log, so Outbox Mode reminders are visible too. Repeat reminders require confirmation to reduce accidental duplicates.
02

Use the Schedule Board

  1. Click Schedule in the sidebar.
  2. Use the top cards to jump to Overdue, Today, Next 7 Days, or Later.
  3. Search or filter by Client, Project, or Status when you need a narrower view.
  4. Turn on Include Completed when you need completed installs shown alongside the schedule.
  5. Click Open Job → to work from the full installation record.
Schedule is a date-focused view of the same jobs already in DispatchProof. It does not change job status or create a separate calendar record.
03

Assign a Crew to an Install

  1. Create a new job or open an active job and click Edit Job Details.
  2. Find Crew Assignment.
  3. Choose an optional Crew / Field Lead, Planned Crew Size, and the Internal Crew or Subcontractors planned for the install.
  4. Save the job. The assignment appears in the internal Field Assignment panel and on Schedule.
  5. Search Schedule by an assigned crew member or subcontractor name when you need to find their work.
Crew Assignment is internal DispatchProof planning information. It is included in backups and Schedule exports, but it is not shown on public readiness links or client-facing installation reports.
04

Check Crew Coverage

  1. Open Schedule.
  2. Use the Crew Assigned and Crew Unassigned cards to see coverage at a glance.
  3. Use Crew Coverage in Search & Filter when you want Assigned or Unassigned installs only.
  4. Upcoming installs without a named crew also surface in Needs Attention.
  5. Open the job and use Edit Crew Assignment to fill the gap.
A job counts as assigned when it has a named internal crew member or subcontractor. Planned Crew Size by itself does not count as a named assignment.
05

Build and Use Crew & Subcontractors

  1. Open Crew in the sidebar.
  2. Add each reusable person once and choose Internal Crew or Subcontractor. Subcontractors can also have an optional Company Name.
  3. On New Job or Edit Job Details, pick internal crew and subcontractors from the same field-assignment picker.
  4. Use Custom Crew only for temporary people or subs you do not need to save and schedule again.
  5. For subcontractors, open the directory record to store insurance, W-9s, MSAs, licenses, and other paperwork. Add expiration dates and warning windows so DispatchProof can flag documents that need attention.
  6. Deactivate a directory record when it should stop appearing on new-job pickers. Existing assignments stay preserved. Permanently delete only when there is no linked job history.
V2.41 keeps all existing records as Internal Crew. Subcontractors use the same availability, conflict detection, job history, and scheduling rules as internal crew.
4A

Find a Subcontractor

  1. From an active job, open Find a Subcontractor in Field Assignment, or open it from Crew & Subcontractors.
  2. Choose the trade — including Snow Plowing / Snow Removal — verify the location, set the radius, and use Balanced tolerance first.
  3. Review the nearby businesses and their Search Match score. This is a trade-fit search score, not a quality rating.
  4. Use Save & Assign to This Job when searching from a job, or save the business to the directory only.
  5. The saved business becomes a normal Subcontractor record and uses the existing schedule, conflict, availability, Field Update, and job-assignment workflows.
Search results are discovery leads only. DispatchProof does not certify or qualify a contractor; confirm credentials, insurance, scope, price, and availability before hiring.
06

Resolve a Crew Conflict

  1. DispatchProof checks active jobs for the same internal crew member or subcontractor assigned to more than one install on the same date.
  2. Open Schedule and use Crew Conflicts or Crew Coverage → Conflicts Only.
  3. The affected Schedule rows show the crew member and the other job creating the conflict.
  4. The same warning appears in Needs Attention and in the job's internal Field Assignment panel.
  5. Open one of the jobs and change the installation date or crew assignment to clear the conflict.
Conflict detection uses reusable Crew & Subcontractor assignments on active jobs. Completed jobs do not create conflicts, and nothing about crew conflicts appears on client-facing or public reports.
07

Manage Crew / Subcontractor Availability

  1. Open Crew and select an internal crew member or subcontractor.
  2. In Availability, enter the Start Date, End Date, and reason.
  3. DispatchProof keeps those dates on the crew profile until you remove them.
  4. If that person is assigned to an active install during the unavailable range, Schedule, Job Detail, and Needs Attention show an internal warning.
  5. Use Schedule → Crew Unavailable to isolate affected jobs, then change the crew assignment or installation date.
Completed jobs do not create availability warnings. Crew/subcontractor availability stays internal and never appears on public readiness, arrival, or client report pages.
08

Check Staffing Gaps

  1. Enter a Planned Crew Size when creating or editing an installation.
  2. DispatchProof compares that target with the unique named people in Crew / Installers / Subcontractors, including the Crew Lead when needed.
  3. If fewer people are assigned than planned, the job shows Staffing Gap with Planned, Assigned, and Crew Members Needed.
  4. Open Schedule and use Staffing Gaps or Crew Coverage → Staffing Gaps Only to isolate short-staffed jobs.
  5. The same internal warning appears in Needs Attention and the job's Field Assignment panel.
Jobs without a Planned Crew Size are not treated as staffing gaps. Completed jobs are excluded, and staffing information never appears on public or client-facing reports.
09

Export or Print the Schedule

  1. Open Schedule.
  2. Apply any search, Client, Project, Status, date-window, or Include Completed filters you want.
  3. Click Export CSV to download exactly the installs in the current view.
  4. Click Print / Save PDF for a clean print-friendly version of the same schedule.
  5. In the browser print window, choose a printer or Save as PDF.
Schedule exports do not change any DispatchProof data. They are snapshots of the filtered Schedule view at the time you export or print.
10

Close a Client Report

  1. Open a single-job or combined client report.
  2. Use Close Report beside Print / Save PDF when you are finished.
  3. If the report was opened from DispatchProof, the report tab closes or returns you to the prior page.
Close Report does not change the report or revoke its secure link. It is only a navigation convenience.
11

Use Schedule Alerts

  1. Open Dashboard.
  2. Use the schedule chips to see Overdue, Today, Next 7 Days, or Later installs.
  3. You can combine a schedule window with Search, Client, Project, or Status filters.
  4. Look for Overdue / Today / Next 7 Days labels beside installation dates in the job list.
The schedule alerts do not change job status. They only help prioritize active jobs by installation date.
12

Export Jobs to CSV

  1. Open Dashboard or Completed Jobs.
  2. Apply any Search, Client, Project, Schedule, or Status filters you want.
  3. Click Export CSV.
  4. Open the downloaded file in Excel, Google Sheets, or another spreadsheet app.
The export follows the current page filters. Clear All first when you want every job in that section.
13

Find a Job on the Dashboard

  1. Open Dashboard.
  2. Use Search for a job name, site, contact, client, project, project number, or contact email.
  3. Optionally choose a Client, Project, or Status.
  4. Click Apply Filters.
  5. Use Clear All to return to every active installation.
The status tiles above the filters also work as quick status filters.
14

Create a Job

  1. Click New Job in the sidebar.
  2. Optionally choose a Client and Project.
  3. Enter the Job Name, Project / Site, and Installation Date.
  4. Enter the site contact name and email.
  5. Review the standard readiness checklist and reminder settings.
  6. Use Other → Project-Specific Requirements for anything unique to this installation. Click + Add Another when more than one custom requirement is needed.
  7. Click Create Job.
Tip: jobs can stay unassigned and be added to a client/project later.
15

Edit Job Details

  1. Open the job.
  2. Click Edit Job Details at the top.
  3. Update the job/site name, installation date, contact information, or reminder settings.
  4. Click Save Job Details.
  5. Review Job Activity to see exactly what changed.
This does not replace Client/Project assignment, readiness evidence, arrival records, reports, or Office Notes.
16

Duplicate as a Separate New Job

  1. Use this only when you are creating a separate installation/job that happens to reuse the same setup.
  2. Open the existing job and click Duplicate as New Job.
  3. Review the copied client/project, site, contact, checklist, and reminder settings.
  4. Choose the new Installation Date and edit anything that changed.
  5. Click Create Job.
Return visit? Do not duplicate the job. On the original job, use Start Return Visit / Next Mobilization so prior evidence stays in Mobilization History and the crew is not falsely double-booked.
17

Understand Job Status

NO RESPONSE

Readiness has not been confirmed yet.

REVIEW

A response needs office review.

READY

The site is confirmed ready to dispatch.

BLOCKED

The site is not ready.

ON SITE

Installer arrival was successful.

COMPLETED

The installation has been marked complete.

Daily Use

Everyday Workflows

The core actions used to manage a live installation.

18

Send a Readiness Request

  1. Open the job.
  2. Open the Readiness Request page.
  3. Review the site contact and checklist.
  4. Click Generate Readiness Email.
  5. When Email Outbox Mode is enabled, confirm it appears in Email Outbox.
  6. The site contact opens the secure readiness link and submits the checklist.
19

Record Installer Arrival

  1. Wait until the job is READY.
  2. Open the job and find the installer arrival section.
  3. Use Copy Installer Link for the field installer, or Record Internally.
  4. Choose Site Ready when the installer can proceed.
  5. Add the reporter name and photos if needed.
  6. Submit. The job moves to ON SITE.
20

Record a Failed Mobilization

  1. Open the installer arrival form.
  2. Choose Site Not Ready.
  3. Select the failure reason(s).
  4. Enter Crew Affected and Hours Lost.
  5. Add at least two arrival photos.
  6. Submit to create the failed mobilization record and report.
Equipment affected and installer notes are optional. If the crew must return to this same installation, use Start Return Visit / Next Mobilization on the same job rather than duplicating it.
21

Find a Completed Job

  1. Click Completed Jobs in the sidebar.
  2. Search by job, site, contact, client, project, project number, or contact email.
  3. Optionally filter by Client or Project.
  4. Click Apply Filters.
  5. Use Clear All to return to the full completed-job history.
22

Complete a Job

  1. Open a job that is ON SITE.
  2. Click Mark Job Complete.
  3. Confirm the action.
  4. The job moves to Completed Jobs.
  5. The installer link is revoked, while history and evidence stay preserved.
23

Add an Internal Job Note

  1. Open the job.
  2. Scroll to Office Notes.
  3. Enter the update, call note, scheduling detail, or other internal information.
  4. Click Save Internal Note.
  5. The note is stamped with the signed-in user and time.
Internal notes never appear in client reports or public links.
24

Read the Job Activity Trail

  1. Open any job.
  2. Scroll to Job Activity.
  3. Each event shows the action, time, and person/type responsible.
  4. Use it to verify job creation, readiness, arrivals, assignments, reports, and completion.
25

Use Field Updates & Daily Progress

  1. Open the job and click Field Updates.
  2. Choose an assigned installer/subcontractor or enter a recipient manually.
  3. Type the PM instructions/request, choose whether to share the site contact, and select only the Job/Project/Client documents the field person should be able to open.
  4. Generate the secure Field Access Link. The field person can review shared drawings/instructions, respond with notes/photos, record a voice update, or submit a Daily Progress Log.
  5. Voice updates keep the original audio with the job. When an OpenAI transcription API key is configured, DispatchProof also converts the recording to text automatically.
  6. Daily Progress keeps work dates, progress notes, optional crew/hours/issues, photos, and voice evidence in chronological job history.
  7. Daily Progress is automatically available in the job's Client Report.
Field Updates are separate from Readiness and Installer Arrival. A field response never changes or unlocks mobilization evidence.
26

Use Email Outbox

  1. Click Email Outbox.
  2. Open any generated email.
  3. Review the recipient, subject, branded preview, and secure shared link.
  4. In Email Outbox Mode, OUTBOX means DispatchProof generated the email but did not send it externally.
27

Review Job Communication History

  1. Open an installation.
  2. Find Communication History below the Installation Report panel.
  3. Review the generated type, recipient, status, timestamp, and subject.
  4. Click View Preview → to open the exact generated message.
  5. Use Email Outbox when you need communication across every job.
This job-level history includes Readiness Requests, Reminders, Field Update Requests, and single-job Client Reports. Combined client/project reports remain in Email Outbox because they cover more than one installation.
Practice Workspace

Training & Simulation

Use sample scenarios to practice operational decisions without touching live company work.

T1

Practice in Training Mode

  1. Open Training from the sidebar.
  2. Choose an assigned scenario and read the simulated job facts.
  3. Select the action you would take in DispatchProof.
  4. Every answer is clearly marked Correct or Not Quite. A Not Quite answer gives a coaching note and keeps you on that question until you choose the best action.
  5. Complete each step to finish the scenario.
Safe sandbox: Training Mode never sends real email, changes live jobs, alters crew schedules, creates public links, consumes contractor-search/routing credits, or changes client reports.
T2

Assign Training as an Administrator

  1. Open Training.
  2. Choose an active user.
  3. Assign one scenario or the Complete Starter Track.
  4. Review progress from the Admin Training Manager.
  5. Use Download All Results CSV for the complete company training record, or download an individual trainee's CSV from the same panel.
  6. Use Reset when the trainee should practice a scenario again.
Training retries are coaching opportunities, not a pass/fail grade. Each scenario draws from a larger question bank in a shuffled order, and Reset/Practice Again reshuffles the run so trainees learn the workflow instead of memorizing a sequence.
Organization

Clients & Projects

Group multiple installation jobs without changing the individual job workflow.

28

Create a Client

  1. Click Clients & Projects.
  2. Click New Client.
  3. Enter the client name and optional primary contact details.
  4. Save the client.
29

Create a Project

  1. Open the client.
  2. Click New Project.
  3. Enter the Project Name.
  4. Optionally add a project number, location, and notes.
  5. Save the project.
29A

Optimize a Rollout Route

  1. Open Clients & Projects, then open the rollout/project.
  2. Click Route Optimization.
  3. For a large rollout, use Download Job Import Template, paste the client/store list into the CSV, and upload it to create the jobs in bulk. Required columns are Job Name, Route / Site Address, and Installation Date.
  4. Know the limits: one CSV can import up to 250 jobs, while one optimized route can include up to 40 active jobs. Projects with more than 40 jobs can still be imported; select the group you want included in the current route.
  5. Enter the crew's starting location, select the active jobs to include, and correct any route addresses if needed.
  6. Choose whether the crew should return to the starting location, then click Optimize Selected Jobs.
  7. Review the numbered stop order, road mileage, drive time, and route map.
  8. If field knowledge requires a different sequence, use the up/down arrows and click Save Manual Order & Recalculate.
  9. Use Download Route CSV to keep or share the saved stop order, addresses, mileage, and drive-time summary outside DispatchProof.
Saved route plans are private to the user who created them. Route addresses are used for planning only and do not rewrite the job's Site / Project field.
30

Assign an Existing Job

  1. Open the job.
  2. Find Client & Project.
  3. Open Change Assignment.
  4. Select the client and project.
  5. Click Save Assignment.
Choosing a project automatically uses that project’s client.
31

Create a Job from a Project

  1. Open Clients & Projects.
  2. Open the client, then the project.
  3. Click New Job.
  4. The Client and Project fields are preselected automatically.
  5. Finish the normal New Job form and create the job.
Files & References

Documents

Store files at the client, project, or individual job level and find them from one searchable center.

32

Use Job Documents

  1. Open the installation.
  2. Click Job Documents at the top of the job, or scroll to the Job Documents section.
  3. Choose the scope, drawing, work order, spreadsheet, or other file that belongs only to that installation.
  4. Click Upload Document.
  5. Use Download whenever the office needs the file again. Owner and Administrator can delete files when needed.
Job Documents stay with that specific installation and follow the job's Personal / Team access. They are internal only, included in backups, and never appear in client reports or public links.
33

Use Project Documents

  1. Open Clients & Projects, then open the project.
  2. Find Project Documents.
  3. Upload the project-wide scope, drawing, spreadsheet, or reference file.
  4. Every authorized job assigned to that project can show the file under Project Reference Files.
  5. Operations can upload/download. Owner and Administrator can also delete project files.
Project Documents are internal only, follow Project workspace access, are included in authorized backups, and never appear in public or client reports.
34

Use Client Documents

  1. Open Clients & Projects, then open the client.
  2. Find Client Documents.
  3. Upload a client-wide standard, agreement, requirement, drawing, or reference file.
  4. Authorized projects and jobs under that client can show it under Client Reference Files.
  5. Operations can upload/download. Owner and Administrator can also delete client files.
Client Documents are internal only, follow Client workspace access, are included in authorized backups, and never appear in public or client-facing reports.
35

Use Document Center

  1. Click Documents in the sidebar.
  2. Search by filename, client, project, job, site, or uploader.
  3. Filter by Client, Project, Job, or a specific client/project.
  4. Click Download to get the file.
  5. Use Open Client →, Open Project →, or Open Job → to manage it at its original location.
Document Center does not move or duplicate files. It is a searchable internal index across the Client, Project, and Job document libraries the signed-in user is authorized to access.
36

Quick Upload a Document

  1. Click Documents.
  2. In Quick Upload, choose Client Document, Project Document, or Job Document.
  3. Choose the matching Client, Project, or Job.
  4. Select the file and click Upload Document.
  5. DispatchProof stores the file at that original level and immediately adds it to Document Center.
Quick Upload uses the same 20 MB limit, internal-only permissions, Activity Log, and Backup & Restore behavior as uploading from the Client, Project, or Job page.
Reporting

Client Reports

Share a secure branded report for one installation.

37

Share a Single-Job Client Report

  1. Open the job.
  2. Find Installation Report.
  3. Click Client Report & Email.
  4. Preview the secure report first.
  5. Enter the client recipient and generate/send the report email.
  6. When Email Outbox Mode is enabled, review the generated message in Email Outbox.
38

Revoke a Client Report Link

  1. Open Client Report & Email.
  2. Find Secure Report Link.
  3. Click Rotate Secure Link.
  4. Confirm the warning.
  5. The old link stops working immediately and a new link replaces it.

Safety

Backup & Restore

Protect the live database, evidence uploads, users, clients, projects, and history.

49

Respond to a Backup Reminder

  1. When the Dashboard shows Backup recommended or Backup overdue, click Back Up Now.
  2. Review the Backup Status and record counts.
  3. Click Download Backup ZIP.
  4. Save the ZIP somewhere you can find again.
  5. The Dashboard reminder clears automatically after the fresh backup is created.
DispatchProof recommends a backup after 3 days and marks it overdue after 7 days. Always make a fresh backup before a deploy or restart.
50

Download a Backup

  1. Open Backup & Restore.
  2. Review the record counts.
  3. Click Download Backup ZIP.
  4. Save it somewhere you can find again.
  5. Always make a fresh backup before deploying a new DispatchProof version.
51

Restore After a Render Reset

  1. Open Backup & Restore.
  2. Select the latest DispatchProof backup ZIP.
  3. Run the restore.
  4. Wait for the verified success message.
  5. Confirm the job count, branding, users, clients/projects, and evidence returned.
The restore verification protects against the older SQLite sidecar-file problem.
