## After Render deploys

1. Confirm Version 2.11.
2. Restore the latest validated V2.10 backup if Render resets.
3. Open an active test job.
4. Confirm **Edit Job Details** appears at the top beside Duplicate Job.
5. Click Edit Job Details.
6. Confirm current job/site, date, contact, and reminder values are prefilled.
7. Change one harmless test value, such as Project / Site or Contact Phone.
8. Click **Save Job Details**.
9. Confirm the updated value appears on Job Detail.
10. Scroll to Job Activity and confirm **Job Details Updated** lists the old and new values.
11. Confirm readiness/evidence and Office Notes remain unchanged.
12. Open Help & Tutorials and search `edit`; confirm **Edit Job Details** appears.
13. Save a fresh V2.11 backup after validation.
