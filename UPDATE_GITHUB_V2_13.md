## After Render deploys

1. Confirm Version 2.13.
2. Restore the latest validated V2.12 backup if Render resets.
3. Open **Completed Jobs** and open a completed test installation.
4. As Owner/Admin, confirm **Reopen Job** appears in the completed-job banner.
5. Click Reopen Job and confirm the warning.
6. Confirm the job returns to **ON SITE**.
7. Confirm it disappears from Completed Jobs and returns to the active Dashboard.
8. Scroll to Job Activity and confirm **Job Reopened** appears.
9. Confirm readiness, arrival evidence, reports, and Office Notes are still intact.
10. Sign in as an Operations user, open a completed job if one exists, and confirm **Reopen Job** is not available.
11. Open Help & Tutorials and search `reopen`; confirm **Reopen a Completed Job** appears for Owner/Admin.
12. Save a fresh V2.13 backup after validation.
