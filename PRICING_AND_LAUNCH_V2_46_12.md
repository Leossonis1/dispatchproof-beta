# DispatchProof Launch Pricing & Positioning

## Public price

**$50/month per company**

Initial launch offer:
- Up to 10 office users
- All core DispatchProof features included
- No setup fee
- No per-feature tiers
- 30-day trial can be offered without requiring a credit card

Keep pricing simple during launch. Do not create Basic/Pro/Enterprise feature gates until customer behavior proves a real need.

## Core positioning

**Your back office tracks the job. DispatchProof runs it.**

DispatchProof is construction field-execution and deployment software for teams responsible for getting jobs ready, staffed, coordinated, documented, and completed in the real world.

It is not accounting software, estimating software, payroll, or another ERP.

## 30-second pitch

DispatchProof gives construction operations teams one place to prove a job is ready before the crew leaves, coordinate crews and subcontractors, catch scheduling conflicts, keep job documents and field evidence together, communicate with the field, and document what actually happened on site. It works alongside the back-office tools a company already uses instead of trying to replace them.
